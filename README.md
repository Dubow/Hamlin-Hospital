# Hamlin Hospital Website

## HAMLIN HOSPITAL-NAIROBI 
The website was built to provide patients and visitors with easy access to essential hospital information, including services offered, departments, and contact details.

---

## 🏥 Project Background

Hospitals require reliable and user-friendly digital platforms to communicate with patients and the general public.  
This website was designed to serve as an informational platform for Hamlin Hospital, supporting its online presence by presenting hospital details in a clear and structured manner.

The project reflects real-world requirements of a functioning healthcare facility in Nairobi.

---

## ✨ Features

- Home page introducing the hospital
- About Us section with hospital background
- Services and departments information
- Contact page with hospital details
- Simple and intuitive navigation
- Responsive layout for different devices
- Clean and professional healthcare-focused design

---

## 🧰 Technologies Used

- **HTML5** – Semantic structure and content organization  
- **CSS3** – Styling, layout, and responsiveness  
- **JavaScript** – Basic interactivity (if applicable)
